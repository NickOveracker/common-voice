# Privacy notice (draft)

**This is a placeholder for the technical trial.** The community will write the real notice.

During the trial, please add only test recordings to this site.

- Only members can use this site. Recordings and transcriptions can be seen only by members who have signed in.
- Recordings, speaker details and consent records are kept on the server that runs this site. They are not sent to outside services.
- A speaker's real name and notes can be seen only by whoever added the speaker, the speaker themselves, and moderators.
- Only admins can export (download) data, and an export includes only what speakers consented to.
- To delete your account or change your email address, contact an admin.
